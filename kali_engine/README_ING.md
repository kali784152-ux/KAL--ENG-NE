# 3D Fractal Engine & Ray Marching Studio

An advanced, real-time 3D Ray Marching and Fractal exploration engine built with Python, Pygame, and ModernGL. It features high-performance Shader/GLSL rendering, dynamic lighting, gravitational effects, and destruction mechanics.

## 👤 Author & Maintainer

* **Developer:** Kali
* **GitHub Profile:** [kali784152-ux](https://github.com/kali784152-ux)

## 🌟 Key Features

* **10 Mathematical Fractals:** Includes Giant Mandelbox, Menger Sponge, Mandelbulb, Apollonian Gasket, Quaternion Julia, and more.
* **Destruction Force Sphere (`1` Key):** Spawns a gravitational sphere in front of the camera that deforms, tears apart, and dissolves fractals.
* **Interactive Light Control (Mouse Scroll):** Smoothly adjust or turn off illumination and dynamic shadows on the fly.
* **Cinematic Effects:** Features Depth of Field (DOF), Gravitational Lensing, Chromatic Aberration, ACES Tone Mapping, and Bloom.
* **12K High-Res Screenshots (`P` Key):** Captures high-definition rendered images directly to disk.
